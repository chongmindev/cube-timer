# cube-timer: csTimer, but better

Captured 2026-10-23. Not a spec yet, just the ideas to build from.

## Why
csTimer works, but the UI is bad: little thought for UX, and it's hard to navigate.

## Ideas
1. **Real UI/UX.** A clean, easy-to-navigate interface. This is the core reason the project exists.
2. **WCA ID connection.** Link your WCA ID and show your official stats (PRs, rankings, comps) next to your practice times.
3. **Shareable times/scrambles.** A much more visually appealing way to share a time or scramble (e.g. a nice image card instead of copied text).
4. **Solve reconstruction.** Reconstruct a solve after the fact (moves, steps, splits).

## Open questions (for when building starts)
- Platform: web app, mobile, or both?
- How reconstruction works: typed in by hand, or captured from a smart cube over Bluetooth?
- Which events matter first (3x3 only, or all WCA events)?
- Import existing csTimer sessions?
